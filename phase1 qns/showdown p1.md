# Phase 1: First Contact — 300 pts

One-on-one against one of our bots. 100 hands, one match per attempt. table_rule reads standard the whole way through.

Clear: finish with a chip delta of +10 or better → 300 points.

That is the whole phase — everything else works exactly as the main guide describes.

One thing to expect: with no cap on bet sizes, results swing hard from attempt to attempt. Only your best attempt counts, so don't rewrite everything after one bad run.
